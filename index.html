Financial Consumer Complaints Analysis
Problem 75,513 complaints over 9 years — but nobody knew which products, states, or processes were causing the most pressure on support teams.

🚉 02 Excel + DAX
UK Railway Station Operations & Performance
Problem Across 9 UK stations, journeys were being delayed and cancelled — but teams had no clear picture of where, when, or why delays were clustered.
Insight Delays are predictable, not random. They peak on Wednesdays at 8–9 AM. Manchester Piccadilly averages 60 min delays. Signal failure is the #1 cause at every station.
Result Recommended signal upgrades at 7 priority stations, automatic refunds for Off-Peak passengers, and boosted staffing on peak-delay windows.

⛽ 03 Excel
H₂ Fuel Station — Sales & Performance Analysis
Problem Station management needed to know where its revenue was coming from and whether 6 pumps and 5 customer segments were performing efficiently.
Insight Trucks generate 57% of total revenue. Peak sales happen 3–5 PM. Pump 4 was flagged as a consistent underperformer across the full year.
Result Recommended a Truck-First strategy, immediate Pump 4 investigation, pre-stocking for December peaks, and adding high-margin products to grow the 3.68% margin.
